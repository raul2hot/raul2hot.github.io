Privacy Policy

For the Oriole Android app · Last updated 10 August 2026

The short version. Oriole has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your settings, bookmarks and keys stay on your phone. When you search, your search words go to the server you chose, and nothing is contacted at all until you make that choice. When you stream or download, you join a BitTorrent swarm, and your IP address is visible to the other people in it, exactly as it is with any BitTorrent client.

1. Who this is about

Oriole ("the app") is an independent Android application developed by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. It covers the app itself and the optional public-domain library server that we operate. It does not cover other servers you may choose to connect the app to, or the wider BitTorrent network, both of which are outside our control.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not know who you are.
  • We do not show advertising and we include no advertising SDKs.
  • We include no analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not collect an advertising ID.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

4. Information sent when you use the app

a. To the source you choose

Oriole asks on first launch where files should come from, and contacts nothing until you answer. Once you have chosen, searching sends your search words and filters, plus the access key for that server, to that server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Your device's IP address is necessarily visible to it, as it is to any website you visit. If you point the app at someone else's server, that server's operator decides what to do with those requests; their policy applies, not ours.

b. The public-domain library (operated by us)

If you use the built-in library, the above requests come to a server we run. It receives your search words and your IP address. We keep short-lived operational logs for security and to keep the service running, we do not use them to build profiles of users, and we do not share them with anyone except where we are legally required to. We do not link searches to any identity, because we do not have one for you.

c. AI search and tracked topics (optional, off by default)

AI search only works if you supply your own OpenRouter API key. When you use it, your question and the search results being considered are sent to OpenRouter and the model provider you selected, under your own account and their privacy policies. If you never enter a key, no such request is ever made.

Tracked topics use the same path while you are not watching. A topic is a query you save so the app can re-run it on a schedule you choose. Each run sends that query, and the results being considered, to OpenRouter exactly as an AI search does — in the background, on your own key and at your own cost. Topics exist only if you create them, they can be paused or deleted individually, and the app stops checking on its own when the phone has gone unused for a while, so a forgotten topic does not keep spending. With no key and no topics, nothing runs in the background.

d. The BitTorrent network

Streaming or downloading a torrent means participating in a peer-to-peer swarm. This is how BitTorrent works: your IP address is visible to the other peers and trackers in that swarm, and theirs to you. Those peers are unrelated third parties, not us, and we neither collect nor receive that information. Peer and tracker traffic is also not encrypted the way a website is, so the fact that you are using BitTorrent can be visible to anyone who can observe your network. If any of this matters for your situation, use a VPN or do not use BitTorrent software.

5a. The local media server

To play a file while it is still arriving, the app runs a small HTTP server on the phone itself. It listens on the local network as well as on the phone's own loopback address, because a television you cast to fetches the file over the network and cannot read the app's memory. Every request to it must carry a long random token that is generated afresh each time the server starts, so other devices on the same Wi-Fi cannot browse or download what you are watching. The server serves only files the app is currently working with, and it reaches nothing outside the app's own storage.

6. Children

Oriole is not directed at children and is not designed for use by children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no account and no profile for you, there is normally nothing personal of yours for us to export or delete. You can remove everything the app holds by clearing the cache in Settings and uninstalling it. If you believe we hold information about you and want it removed, write to us at the address below and we will act on it.

8. Changes to this policy
